# Curriculum and content sources

Last reviewed: 20 October 2026.

## Primary official sources

1. [Karnataka School Examination and Assessment Board (KSEAB)](https://kseab.karnataka.gov.in) — official examination notifications, SSLC model papers and board resources.
2. [Karnataka Textbook Society](https://textbooks.karnataka.gov.in/english) — official state textbook portal and medium-specific textbooks.
3. [Karnataka official results portal](https://karresults.nic.in) — official examination results service.

## Secondary cross-checks used during initial structuring

- [Karnataka SSLC syllabus overview](https://www.pw.live/kseab/exams/karnataka-sslc-syllabus) — used only to cross-check the 2026–27 subject-level structure.
- [Social Science syllabus overview](https://byjus.com/kseeb/class-10-social-science-syllabus/) — used only to cross-check discipline grouping.
- [Science syllabus overview](https://byjus.com/kseeb/class-10-science-syllabus/) — used only to cross-check chapter grouping.
- [2026 model-paper reporting](https://www.jagranjosh.com/articles/karnataka-sslc-model-question-papers-2026-download-kseab-subject-wise-sample-paper-with-answer-key-1800006559-1) — used to confirm that current model sets are published through KSEAB; students are directed to the official portal.

Content derived from these sources is paraphrased and summarised, not reproduced. Content was rephrased for compliance with licensing restrictions.

## Publication rules

- The official KSEAB portal and current medium-specific textbook are the controlling references. If a secondary source disagrees, do not publish until KSEAB/KTBS is checked.
- `questionBank` contains original, syllabus-aligned practice questions. It never labels them as previous-year or official questions.
- Official question papers are linked at their official source rather than copied into this repository. Before displaying a direct PDF link, a reviewer must record year, subject code, medium, set, source URL, verification date and publication permission.
- Prose, poetry and textbook pages are not reproduced. The app provides original skills notes and links learners to authorised textbooks.
- Third-language content must match each student's registered subject; Hindi must not be assumed for every learner.
- Before a pilot, a qualified teacher must verify chapter names, order, translations, formulas, option keys and marking explanations against the official 2026–27 documents.

## Content status

The Mathematics, Science and Social Science structures and concise notes are an initial reviewed baseline. They are useful for development and teacher review, but the application must not describe them as formally KSEAB-approved. Language sections intentionally focus on transferable grammar, reading and writing skills because prescribed prose/poetry must come from authorised current textbooks.
